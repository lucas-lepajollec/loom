# Code of conduct

## Our commitment

We are committed to a respectful, welcoming and harassment-free community. Be constructive, assume good faith, respect different experiences and focus criticism on ideas and work rather than people.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized attention, deliberate intimidation, doxxing, publishing private information and sustained disruption are not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to the maintainers at `contact@lucas-homelab.fr` or through GitHub private reporting channels. Maintainers may edit, reject or remove contributions and temporarily or permanently restrict participation when necessary to protect the community.
